mojimask: emoji masks for party videos and photos
=================================================

Covers every face in a video or photo with an emoji (or blur, pixelate, solid), one at a time.
Everything happens on your Mac; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Needs: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later, ~2 GB free disk space.


1. Install
   Unzip, then drag "Mojimask" into your Applications folder.


2. First launch (one time only)
   This app is free and open source and isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate,
   so macOS asks you to confirm it once:

   - Double-click "Mojimask". macOS says it can't verify the developer. Click Done.
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
     next to the message about Mojimask. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

   (Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Mojimask.app" )


3. Use it
   Choose video or photo…   Pick a clip or a photo (or drop it on the window). The scan takes a few
                   minutes per minute of video, a few seconds for a photo; the window shows what the
                   computer sees while it works. A photo is saved back as a photo (JPEG).

   After the scan  Every face we found is already covered. Pick a look, then "Looks good: export",
                   or "Refine the covers" to check the boxes we weren't sure about. The steps along the
                   top (Decisions, People, Edit, Export) can be opened in any order, any time.

   Decisions       The boxes we weren't sure about, one question each ("Is the green box a face?",
                   "Is the green box the same person as the grey one?", ...). Click an answer or press
                   1 / 2 / 3. "To answer" lists what's left, "Answered" what you've said (change it there).
                   Anything you leave unanswered stays covered.

   People          Everyone we counted as a person, each with one emoji. Masked / Unmasked leaves
                   someone visible (yourself, friends who agreed); add a name, or click their emoji to
                   change it. Changes save as you go.

   Edit            The editor: the whole video with its covers.
                     Scrub         drag along the timeline at the bottom; arrow keys move one frame
                                   (Shift: ten), Space plays
                     Select        click an emoji; E changes it; U masks / shows that person
                     Cover with    emoji, blur, pixelate or solid, and the emoji set, on the right;
                                   "Match scene lighting" gives emoji the scene's light
                     Add a face    drag a box around a missed face; [ and ] start / end a box there,
                                   S splits a box where it changes, Del: face / not a face,
                                   K undoes your correction at this frame
                   "Keyboard shortcuts" (or ?) on the right lists every key. Cmd+Z undoes.

   Export          Switch to Export at the top. Pick the cover style and a format:
                     Original          same shape and size as your clip
                     Vertical 9:16     Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Stories
                     Portrait 4:5      Instagram / Facebook feed
                     Square 1:1        square posts
                     Landscape 16:9    YouTube, X, Facebook, LinkedIn
                   "Crop to fill" or "Whole frame, blurred sides"; optional start/end times.
                   The preview shows exactly what will be saved. Export… asks where to save it
                   (default: next to the original). Switch back to Edit any time.
                   "Higher-risk footage" (a protest, say) covers whole heads with solid shapes and
                   asks you to answer every open question in Decisions before exporting.

   The exported .mp4 (H.264 + AAC) has no hidden location, date or device details.
   Still check the sound (voices, names) and things like tattoos, signs and name badges.

   Next clip       Click "Projects" at the top left. Earlier projects stay listed there.


4. Storage
   Projects live in ~/Library/Application Support/Mojimask/Projects. The start screen lists
   each project's size: "work" (your answers, edits and the scan, small), "cache" (preview frames
   and analysis, rebuilt when you open the project again) and, for a video dropped onto the window,
   "video" (the app's own copy of it). Clear cache or Delete (to the Trash) there.
   Your original videos and photos are never moved or changed.
